About Us

Parcel Pilot was founded by Aloyshia and Indhupriya, two friends who saw how confusing and stressful parcel delivery could be for small businesses and everyday customers. Starting with a single bike and a spreadsheet, they built a courier service focused on honest tracking, fair pricing, and on-time delivery — with the goal of making the courier space simpler and more reliable for everyone.
